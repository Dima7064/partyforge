# Промпты для генерации изображений PartyForge

Стиль (добавляй в конец каждого промпта):

> dark fantasy, oil painting style, dramatic lighting, highly detailed, cinematic, medieval setting, muted colors with gold accents, trending on ArtStation

## Портреты персонажей (768×1152, 2:3)

Шаблон: `Portrait of a {race} {class}, {gender}, {age}, {distinctive features}, bust shot, looking at camera`

## Портреты NPC (512×512, 1:1)

Шаблон: `Portrait of a {role} NPC, {description}, square composition, centered face`

## Локации (1280×720, 16:9)

Шаблон: `Wide shot of a {location_type}, {description}, fantasy setting, no characters in frame`

## Карта региона (1754×1240, A4 landscape, 150 DPI)

`Hand-drawn fantasy map of a region, parchment style, mountains, rivers, forests, cities marked with icons, compass rose, ink drawing, aged paper, landscape composition for A4 printing, 1754x1240 aspect ratio`

## Террейн (1240×1754, A4 portrait, 150 DPI)

`Top-down battle map on A4 portrait format, {terrain_type}, fantasy style, tabletop RPG map, clear grid overlay 12x12 cells centered with margins, high contrast for printing, 1240x1754 aspect ratio`

Примеры террейна:

- Forest clearing: `Top-down battle map of a forest clearing, mossy ground, fallen logs, 12x12 grid centered with margins, fantasy, A4 portrait`
- Tavern interior: `Top-down battle map of a medieval tavern interior, wooden tables, fireplace, 12x12 grid centered, fantasy, A4 portrait`
- Dungeon: `Top-down battle map of a stone dungeon corridor, torches, rubble, 12x12 grid, fantasy, A4 portrait`
